HORIZON privacy policy

Package com.horizon.app · Terrestrial relations board

HORIZON is a terrestrial relations board. It is local-first. There is no HORIZON account and no HORIZON cloud.

What stays on the device

Domain filters, layer switches, and tracked story ids are stored only in the app's private storage. The desk baseline is bundled with the app. Android backup and device-to-device transfer of this app's data are disabled.

What leaves the device

When the LIVE layer is on, the app downloads public world-news RSS feeds (BBC, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, France 24, UN News) so headlines can be placed on the globe. Those requests do not include an account, a name, or the contents of the board. Headlines are not uploaded anywhere.

What never happens

We do not sell data. We do not run ads. We do not upload pins, filters, or the desk.

Permissions

INTERNET, for the public wire only. HORIZON does not use the microphone, camera, location, or contacts.
